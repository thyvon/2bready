Brand Usage Principles

✓ Recommended

  • Use #183659 as the dominant brand color.
  • Use green for positive actions and success states.
  • Keep application backgrounds light and clean.
  • Use teal sparingly for information and secondary emphasis.
  • Maintain strong contrast and consistent hierarchy.

✕ Avoid

  • Do not make green the primary navigation color.
  • Do not use all three brand colors at equal intensity.
  • Do not place navy text on dark navy backgrounds.
  • Do not use bright colors purely for decoration.
  • Do not introduce unrelated brand colors without a functional reason.
